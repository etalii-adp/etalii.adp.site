## The header icon

`adp-header.png` is the header logo beside "ADP – A Different Perspective": the flat "two perspectives" mark, version 3, with the text plate in green and the diagram plate in white on a transparent background, at 128 × 128 px. It is shown in the dark theme. `adp-header-light.png` is the same mark with the diagram plate in dark grey, shown in the light theme, where the white plate would disappear.
