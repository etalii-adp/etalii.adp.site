The desktop host exports a [causal loop diagram](/adp/tools/systems/causal-loop-diagram/) as one self-contained web page, from the **Export flow page** button on its ribbon. Influence flows along the links as dots, delays hold it, and a heatmap shows where it piles up. Each page below is that export of one example. It works offline, and nothing you change in it is written anywhere.

## What you can do in a page

- Watch influence flow along the links, and see delayed links hold it.
- Switch the heatmap between Backlog, Pressure, Throughput and Level.
- Move the sliders of the external pressures and the dynamics: simulation speed, link strength, the length of a delay, the inertia of a variable and the dot density.
- Click a variable to give it an outside push or a shock up or down.
- Click a link to change its strength, add or remove its delay, or flip its polarity.
- Hover or click a loop to trace it, and read its gain.
- Follow the main variables over the last minute in the chart.
- Pan, zoom and drag variables to another place.

The numbers come from a simple model of the diagram's structure. They are not measurements.
