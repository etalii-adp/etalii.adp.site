Long reads behind ADP. Each analysis looks at a field of practice or a body of work and asks what it means for specialised tools. The source of each is kept in [etalii.adp](https://github.com/etalii-adp/etalii.adp/tree/develop/docs/research) where one exists, and the page here follows it word for word.

- [When thinking outgrows the tool](/adp/docs/research/structuring-insight/): what people complain about when their tools cannot hold the shape of their thinking, knowledge or information, from eleven domains, in seven patterns.
- [Shaping technology while it is being made](/adp/docs/research/constructive-technology-assessment/): constructive technology assessment and its relatives, read for the perspective on development they offer and the diagrams, designers and editors that perspective calls for.
