The analyses behind ADP, each shown as an article. Newer ones are written in [etalii.adp](https://github.com/etalii-adp/etalii.adp/tree/develop/docs/research) and shown here word for word.

- [When thinking outgrows the tool](/adp/docs/research/structuring-insight/): what people complain about when their tools cannot hold the shape of their thinking, knowledge or information, and what that means for ADP.
- [The ontology as the application](/adp/docs/research/ontology-driven-platforms/): how ontology-driven data-fusion platforms work, the view of software development they share, and the tools it suggests for ADP.
- [Shaping technology while it is being made](/adp/docs/research/constructive-technology-assessment/): constructive technology assessment and its relatives, read for the perspective on development they offer and the diagrams, designers and editors that perspective calls for.
