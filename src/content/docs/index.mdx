## What ADP is

Some things are understood better as a picture than as prose, and better as a picture made for that one task than as a general-purpose one. ADP gives each such task its own designer, and keeps what the designer shows in plain files beside the work it describes, so a view can be reviewed, branched and merged like any other text.
